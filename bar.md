# Visual bar / MERSA redesign

Reference: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2674081025095575/ (Solar System UI UX Website Landing Page Design, QClay). Viewed actual video at 2s and 6s; frames and poster in references/. The reference is a visual/motion bar, not a gameplay reference. Original Voyager/Opportunity concept images remain the archival annotation bar.

1. A textured astronomical body occupies at least half the opening frame, with its limb cropped beyond the viewport; no generic left-copy/right-globe hero arrangement.
2. At least two-thirds of every main gameplay frame remains world imagery or manipulable 3D; descriptive prose appears only on demand.
3. Light falls across visible surface relief and resolves into deep shadow; authentic NASA surface imagery must be recognizable, not hidden under an opaque gradient.
4. Use one cold cyan interaction accent and neutral white labels in flight; gold is confined to physical hardware and discovery emphasis. No purple gradients or rounded marketing cards.
5. Camera transitions establish spatial continuity: distant planet -> close surface -> grounded hardware -> components separated in the same scene. Do not cut to an unrelated flat inspector dashboard.
6. Every HUD label anchors to an edge, measured flight state, landing target, or artifact component. Keep long text away from the planet limb and central flight path.
7. Motion has a legible destination: ship responds to controls, target closes as approach succeeds, model parts separate to stable positions and return on assembly. No idle bobbing or decorative motion without a player action.

Comparison protocol: judge visual compositions against cropped unlabeled reference imagery; gameplay is judged against the requested pilot/land/discover/dissect loop, since the reference is not a playable game. Do not claim the reference demonstrates features it does not show.
