# Story

Source: source-material/narrative.docx; extracted text alongside it. Full intended order: prologue → Apollo 11 → Sojourner → Spirit → Opportunity → Voyager 1 / Golden Record → final archive report. Only the first chapter is playable.

The archivist initially sees unidentified geometry and interrupted evidence. Apollo reveals the intelligence, engineering, and vulnerability of the species. Footprints shift the interpretation from machines to living visitors. Mars is a promise, not another implemented chapter.

All alien observations, distant-future setting, received signals, model-confidence percentages, and reconstructed scenes are fictional narrative devices. They do not imply that Apollo hardware currently broadcasts signals or that humanity has disappeared.
