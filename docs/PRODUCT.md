# Phase 1

The visitor is a nonhuman archivist entering the Solar System. Mystery precedes naming. The emotional arc is detachment → curiosity → admiration → recognition of human vulnerability.

Playable scope: acquire a signal, pilot a spacecraft toward the Moon and land within a marked zone, scan an unknown structure, identify Apollo 11, inspect three discoveries, reconstruct biological presence, unlock a Mars signal. Later chapters are data placeholders only.

Three hero interactions: component investigation and exploded Eagle descent stage; align and fire a laser to a retroreflector; expose and reconstruct a footprint from archival evidence. Learning is unlocked by action, not paragraphs. Optional quiet audio and accessible controls support the world rather than overwhelm it.

The direct-flight redesign uses genuine NASA imagery and a NASA-derived descent-stage model. Keyboard, touch and assisted flight share landing limits and retry behavior. Click hardware to separate it in the world; optional evidence/manipulation/source disclosures keep narrow screens focused on the artifact. Mars remains a teaser.
