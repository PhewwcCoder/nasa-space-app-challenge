# Phase 1

The visitor is a nonhuman explorer discovering human artifacts. Playable scope remains prologue, Apollo 11 and a Mars signal teaser only. Mystery precedes naming. The emotional arc is curiosity, discovery and recognition.

The opening is a short cockpit arrival followed by first-person real-time lunar descent. Keyboard, touch, braking and optional assisted flight share the same landing simulation. After touchdown, a third-person explorer follows an authored footprint path using W/up or a held touch control; skip-walk provides an accessible equivalent. Scan the unknown structure to identify Apollo 11. A friendly fictional cat offers one optional random question from a three-question set.

Six discoveries reveal one-line alien interpretations: Eagle, footprints, camera, seismometer, retroreflector, plaque and goodwill disc. Eagle offers a schematic cross-section with selectable structure, engine and landing gear. Its complete historical reconstruction is distinguished from the descent stage left behind. Footprint exposure and laser alignment retain their interactive investigations; three additional clues use brief schematic reveals. All six records unlock: "These objects were not waste. They were evidence." Mars remains a teaser.

Facts have official NASA links; alien conclusions are fiction. Text, technical controls and sources are disclosed progressively. No exact archaeological survey claim. Higgsfield CLI is authenticated but no film generation has been submitted. The current opening uses live game rendering, two instruments and moving procedural 3D gloves. Assisted steering remains active during input. Selecting hardware opens a transparent Miso learning poster; cross-section and separation are directly visible.
